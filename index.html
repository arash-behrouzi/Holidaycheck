What makes a good fit?

The score is a weighted average, not a hotel review or a prediction of availability.

Cost score
100 × (1 − total / (1.5 × your budget)), floored at zero. Over-budget options are excluded.
Comfort score
Your 0–100 assessment. Examples use invented destination-level scores, not verified hotel ratings.
Time score
100 × (1 − one-way door-to-resort minutes / 900), floored at zero.
Different flight legs
When independent outbound and return flights are entered, the time score uses the average of both one-way journeys, including each leg's flight and layover time. Every entered outbound fare is paired with every entered return fare; listed airlines are not a guarantee of route availability.
Door-to-resort time
Airport drive + 120 min check-in + flight including layovers + 45 min arrivals + resort transfer. It is an estimate, not a timetable.
Whole-family cost
Package OR return flights + full-stay hotel, plus return baggage, return transfers, extras, extra meals and drinks, return airport driving at your €/km rate, and parking for nights + 1 days.
Driving holidays
Accommodation + extras + meals + return distance × your car cost per km + return tolls/ferries + full-stay destination parking. No airport charges or flights are included. Journey time is one-way driving plus your break budget.
Corporate benefit
Only an instant discount accepted for this exact offer reduces upfront costs. The percentage applies to your entered eligible supplier amount, with minimum spend and optional cap. Cashback and unaccepted vouchers remain potential savings. Your company's 7% example is not independently verified. No voucher code is stored.
Final-price checks
No live booking provider is connected. Final-price-only results require a manually checked final pre-booking total for the exact itinerary, family and room. Supplier amounts must match after accepted instant discounts. Checks expire after 30 minutes as a recheck reminder; this is not a supplier price guarantee or reservation. Airport travel and estimated meal budgets are not supplier-confirmed prices.
Meal budgets in examples
No meals: €30 per adult and €18 per child per night. Full board: €8 per adult and €5 per child per night for extra drinks and snacks. These are illustrative allowances, not local restaurant prices. For saved quotes, enter your own full-stay extra budget.
Nights and nightly prices
Every whole-number stay from minimum through maximum is considered. The average price per night is the full trip total divided by its actual nights, for the whole family, not a hotel-only rate. Provider searches use the specific number of nights selected in Check the market.
School calendar
Both departure and return must fall within the inclusive holiday dates. Adjacent weekends and school-specific movable days are not assumed. Check your school's calendar.

Routes, seasonal suitability and prices in examples are not checked. All-zero weights produce a score of zero. Money and time are rounded for comparison.
